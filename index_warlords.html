Armor-Piercing, Multi-Role, Astromech

With an excellent mix of durability and firepower, the Incom built T-65 X-wing is as reliable as it is versatile. Built as a general-purpose starfighter the X-wing can engage both lighter craft and larger, more heavily armored gunships and while it has an average top speed, the X-Wings S-foils can help it flee from engagements it cannot win. Its astromech droid can make repairs while it is engaged in combat, benefitting from prolonged dogfighting against craft with low damage output, or craft with debuffed firerate. While the T-65 is an excellent general-purpose craft, it can be outmaneuvered and will struggle in dogfights against dedicated agile, high-damage dogfighters like the Imperial TIE Interceptor, where its self-repair and shielding are overwhelmed. Indeed, for many later Imperial interceptor craft being able to hunt and destroy the versatile X-wing became a design necessity, and later designs such as the Avenger and Hunter will mercilessly beat the X-wing. The X-wing can be used against a variety of targets, but should avoid dedicated dogfighters, or using single, head-on strikes to benefit from its durability when necessary.

Expert Dogfighter, Heavily Armed, Multi-Role, Adv. Armor & Materials, High Capacity Shields, Powerful Engines

The end of a long line of research and prototypes, the TIE Defender incorporates the most advanced technology, sporting light-weight yet extremely durable armor and shielding, in addition to a plethora of weapon systems to engage any foe. Created to be the last word in space-superiority craft, the Empire left no expense spared and no kyber crystal unturned to design the highest performing and one of the most expensive TIE craft ever built. While its added features give the Defender more bulk than its predecessor the TIE Avenger, its heavy armor, powerful standard shields and blistering agility give it enough durability to offset its larger target profile, leaving it vulnerable only to the most intense missile barrages. A vulnerability of the TIE series that even the Defender has been unable to fix.
