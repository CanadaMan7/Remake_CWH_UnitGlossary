Built intentionally as a slow flying fortresses in the days of the Republic to prevent usage by pirates, the heavily armored Gozanti is an extremely durable gunship and transport equipped with repair drones it can use to facilitate repairs to itself or nearby craft. Armed with heavy laser turrets and a forward-mounted torpedo launcher it is easily capable of defending itself against large units and hostile gunships, but its appalling agility and top speed make it an easy target for heavy weaponry. Nevertheless it performs its intended duty admirably as the Gozanti has seen many decades of service and continues to stay relevant in the age of the Empire. Best used to tank endless volleys of small-arms fire and support surrounding craft with its repair drones.
