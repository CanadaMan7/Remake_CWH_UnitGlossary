Very Large Unit, Expert Dogfighter, Heavily Armed, Adv. Armor & Materials, Repair Crew, High Capacity Shielding, Powerful Engines

Of Mandalorian design, the M22-T Krayt Gunship was originally commissioned by Jabba the Hutt as a high-performance commandship for his lieutenantes. Like the Krayt Dragon it was named after, the M22 is a hunter. Its oversized engine provides a high top speed for a gunship and characteristic of Mandalorian design, the Krayt is heavily armed with forward-facing Ion Cannons, rapid-fire Heavy Lasers, HE and AP Missiles, enabling the Krayt to hunt any foe. For self-defence the M22 is well-shielded and equipped with a quad Light Laser turret offering excellent coverage around the gunship. Its head-on barrage may be beastly due to its many forward-facing heavy weapons, but the Krayt needs to lign up a shot due to its lack of turreted weaponry more akin to starfighters than gunships, a major weakness for any large unit. Besides this downside and a vulnerability to missiles and torpedoes that characterizes large units, the Krayt Gunship is the bane of any starfighter or gunship.
